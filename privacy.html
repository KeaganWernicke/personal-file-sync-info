Personal File Sync privacy policy

Effective when published. Personal-use application operated by Keagan Wernicke.

Purpose and information used

Personal File Sync uses Google Drive to store and retrieve selected personal files, backup copies, and information needed to synchronize them, such as filenames, modification times, checksums, and sync history. The connected Google account's email address may be checked to confirm that the intended account was authorized.

The Google Drive permission requested can allow access to files throughout the authorized Drive. The configured sync workflow limits its transfers to its designated folders. The permission itself is broader than that folder configuration.

Storage and access

Synced files and backups are stored on the owner's devices and in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in local application configuration so the utility can connect without requesting the account password. There is no separate application-operated server that receives the files or credentials.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Transfers of Google user data are limited to the owner's authorized devices and Google Drive for the requested synchronization and backup functions.

Retention and control

Synced files, backups, and conflict copies remain until the owner deletes them. Revoking Google access stops future authorized connections but does not remove existing local copies or files already stored in Drive. The owner can revoke this application's access in their Google account settings and remove local credentials, cloud files, and backups separately.

This website

These information pages have no sign-in, upload form, advertising, or application-added analytics. The website hosting provider may process ordinary request information under its own privacy policy. Google processes Drive data under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Google API policy

Personal File Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
